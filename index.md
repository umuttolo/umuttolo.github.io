# Umut Yüksel

I am a Research Fellow at the [Department of Political Science](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/social-historical-sciences/political-science) at University College London and Research Associate at the [Global Governance Centre](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch/globalgovernance) of the Geneva Graduate Institute. Before joining UCL, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellow at the [Department of Political and Social Sciences](https://www.upf.edu/web/politiques) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.

I obtained my PhD (*summa cum laude*) from the Geneva Graduate Institute in November 2019 with a thesis entitled “Bargaining Over Maritime Boundaries in Times of Legal Uncertainty”. Prior to this, I held a pre-doctoral fellowship at the [Department of Political Science](https://politicalscience.stanford.edu/) at Stanford University with the support of the Swiss National Science Foundation, and visiting research fellowships at [Institut Barcelona d’Estudis Internacionals](https://www.ibei.org/ca), the [Centre of Excellence for International Courts and Governance](https://jura.ku.dk/icourts/) at the University of Copenhagen (iCourts), the [Faculty of Law](https://www.upf.edu/web/dret) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, and the [Department of International Relations](https://ir.bilkent.edu.tr/) at Bilkent University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **international security**, **international organizations**, **international law**, and **domestic politics**. Using original data and mixed-methods, I examine how characteristics of international law and domestic political institutions shape the claims that states make and the ways in which they manage their disputes. I work on these queries in various domains, ranging from sovereignty to trade and human rights.
