## Social Science Methods for International Lawyers

I have co-designed this methods sequence with the support of the Department of International Law at the Geneva Graduate Institute, and taught or co-taught the following modules:

- Fundamentals of Social Science Research
- Introduction to Quantitative Data Analysis with R
- Basic Quantitative Methods

We offered this series in Fall 2022 for the European Society of International Law’s Interest Group on Social Sciences and International Law.

[Workshop flyer available on request](mailto:umut.yuksel@graduateinstitute.ch?subject=ESIL%20workshop%20flyer)

I also taught the sessions on Statistics/Quantitative Methods in 2023, 2024, and 2025.
